Classical Symbolic AI Architecture & Pipeline

DocuMind AI implements knowledge-based document question answering without neural embeddings, large language models, or probabilistic hallucination. Knowledge is stored as structured Subject-Predicate-Object (SPO) triples in SQLite, questions are parsed using deterministic grammar rules across 9 canonical categories, and deductive inferences are generated via Modus Ponens forward chaining and goal-directed backward chaining.

01
Document Extraction
PyMuPDF & python-docx extract plain text without neural pre-parsing.
02
SPO Knowledge Base
Rule-based predicate extraction storing canonical triples in SQLite.
03
Grammar Parsing
Deterministic classification mapping queries into StructuredQuery models.
04
Symbolic Reasoning
Forward and backward chaining with R1 to R4 production rules.
05
Answer & Provenance
Templates synthesize verifiable answers with ground citation paths.
